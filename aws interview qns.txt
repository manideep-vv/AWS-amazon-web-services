18. Kafka vs SQS/SNS

Because you know Kafka, interviewers may ask this.

Questions
Kafka vs SQS?
Kafka vs SNS?
When would you choose Kafka?
When would you choose SQS?
What is Kafka better at?
What is SQS better at?

A simple interview answer:

Kafka is generally used for high-throughput event streaming where consumers may independently consume and replay events. SQS is primarily a managed queue for decoupling producers and consumers and distributing work.
